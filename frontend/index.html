Three phases, no way to peek

Every round runs on the clock of the chain. Nobody can look at a price while messages are still being judged.

1 · Bid

Write your message, pick a secret price, lock a fixed deposit. The chain stores only a hash of the price.

2 · Review

The AI gate and the curator check each message against the slot's rules. Refused bidders may appeal once.

3 · Reveal

Cleared bidders reveal. The highest valid price wins and pays the second price. Everyone else gets the deposit back.

The model never sees money

The gate only gets the message and the rules. It cannot be bribed with a price, because the price is not in the room.

Validators must agree

A read is stored only when the validators reach the same gate state. A different answer is not accepted.

Books that balance

Every wei paid in is paid out or held. solvency() proves it, and this site shows the result above.
